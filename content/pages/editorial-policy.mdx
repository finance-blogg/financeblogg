Our aim is to help readers in India understand money matters accurately and without hype. This policy explains how we work.

## Educational, not advisory

We explain how financial products, rules and concepts work. We do not tell individual readers what to buy, and we do not publish tips, return predictions or promises of financial outcomes. Articles are general and do not consider any reader's personal situation.

## Sources

We rely on primary sources wherever possible, including:

- the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC);
- the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI);
- the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI);
- the Income Tax Department, the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs and the GST portal;
- official Union Budget and government publications.

Each article lists its sources at the end. We do not invent statistics, quotes, rates or citations. If we cannot verify a claim, we leave it out or clearly say it is unverified.

## Facts, examples, estimates and opinions

We separate these clearly:

- **Facts** are stated plainly and supported by sources.
- **Illustrative examples** use simplified numbers to explain a concept and are labelled "Illustrative example".
- **Estimates** are labelled as estimates and are not guarantees.
- **Opinions** are labelled "Our view (opinion)".

## Time-sensitive information

Tax rules, interest rates, limits and regulations change, often every year. Where an article depends on a rule that can change, we say which period it applies to, point readers to the official source, and avoid quoting rates that change frequently. We review time-sensitive articles after major announcements such as the Union Budget and RBI policy changes.

## Review process

Every article goes through research, drafting, a fact-check of figures, rules and links against current official sources, and an edit for clarity before publication. Publication from our editorial dashboard requires an explicit fact-check confirmation. Where a named editor has reviewed an article, the review details are shown with the article. We revisit articles when readers report errors or when major regulatory changes affect the topic.

## Use of automated tools

Some articles are drafted with the help of AI writing tools. A human editor reviews, fact-checks and edits every article before publication and remains responsible for its accuracy. AI tools are not used as a substitute for checking official sources.

## Independence and advertising

Our editorial decisions are independent of advertisers. Ads are served automatically by Google AdSense and are clearly labelled "Advertisement". Advertisers do not review or approve articles. We do not accept payment for coverage. If we ever publish sponsored content or use affiliate links, it will be clearly labelled at the top of the article.

## Conflicts of interest

Writers must disclose any financial interest or relationship that could reasonably be seen to affect an article. Where relevant, disclosures appear in the article.

## Updates and corrections

When we make a substantive change to an article, we show an "Updated" date. When we fix a factual error, we follow our [corrections policy](/corrections-policy).
