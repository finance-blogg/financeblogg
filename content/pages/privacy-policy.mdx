## Information we collect

### Information you give us

If you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. We use this only to read and respond to your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information such as account numbers, PAN, Aadhaar numbers, passwords or OTPs.

When email delivery is configured, contact form messages are sent to our inbox through [Resend](https://resend.com). We do not run our own message database; messages live in our email inbox. We keep contact emails only as long as needed to reply and follow up, and we aim to delete routine enquiries within 12 months unless we need them longer for a legal or security reason. If the contact form is not available, you can still reach us at <ContactEmail />.

### Information collected automatically

This website is hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com). Like most websites, the hosting provider may record technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs are used to keep the site secure and working. See [Vercel's Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) for details.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how readers use the site (for example, which pages are viewed and how people navigate). Google Analytics may collect identifiers such as a truncated IP address, device and browser information, and on-site activity. You can learn more in [Google's explanations of Analytics data](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245) and control ads personalisation in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also learn about opting out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

To learn more, see [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where the law requires consent for cookies or personalised ads, such as for visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we use Google's certified consent messaging (AdSense Privacy & messaging) before personalised ads are shown in those regions.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may change the ads you see but will not stop you from reading our articles.
