We try hard to be accurate, but mistakes happen and financial rules change. When we get something wrong, we fix it openly.

## How to report an error

Use our [contact page](/contact) and choose "Correction request", or email <ContactEmail />. Please include:

- the link to the article;
- the statement you believe is wrong or out of date;
- a link to an official or primary source that supports the correction, if you have one.

We aim to review correction requests within <OwnerTodo>number</OwnerTodo> working days.

## How we correct articles

- **Factual errors**: we correct the article and add a dated note at the end describing what was changed, for example: "Correction (date): An earlier version of this article stated X. It has been corrected to Y."
- **Outdated information**: when a rule or rate changes, we update the article and show an "Updated" date at the top.
- **Minor changes**: spelling, grammar, formatting or broken-link fixes that do not change meaning are made without a note.

We do not quietly delete articles to hide errors. If an article is removed for legal reasons or because it can no longer be kept accurate, we will say so where practical.

## Disagreements

If we review a request and decide the article is accurate, we will reply to explain why where possible. We welcome further evidence.
