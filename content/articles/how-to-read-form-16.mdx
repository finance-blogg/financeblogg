**Form 16** is the TDS certificate your employer issues for tax deducted from **salary**. It is the official summary of how much salary they reported, how much tax they deducted, and (in Part B) how they computed that estimate. It is not your income-tax return, and it is not complete if you have interest, capital gains or a second employer.

Use it together with [Form 26AS and AIS](/blog/how-to-read-form-26as-and-ais) and [income-tax basics](/blog/basics-of-income-tax-in-india).

<KeyTakeaways>
- **Part A** is the certificate of tax deducted and deposited against your PAN. **Part B** is the salary computation annexure.
- Match Form 16 with **payslips**, **26AS** and **AIS**. If they disagree, do not file on hope.
- Form 16 covers **this employer’s salary**. FDs, freelance income and another job will not fully appear here.
- The tax in Part B is payroll’s **estimate**. Your ITR can still change the number.
- Keep PDFs for every employer in the year, including a job you left in May.
</KeyTakeaways>

## Part A: the certificate

Part A is generated from the department’s TDS system (TRACES) once the employer has filed TDS returns and deposited tax.

Check:

- your **PAN** and the employer’s **TAN**;
- the **financial year** and period of employment;
- quarterly **TDS deposited** with challan particulars;
- a **receipt / certificate number**.

If Part A shows less tax than your payslips, the employer may not have deposited or filed correctly. That is a payroll problem to raise early. You cannot invent a credit the department does not have.

## Part B: how payroll did the maths

Part B (the annexure) typically walks from gross salary through exemptions and deductions the employer considered, to taxable salary and tax.

Typical blocks:

- gross salary, perquisites, profits in lieu of salary;
- exemptions payroll allowed (for example HRA, if you claimed it under the regime that permits it);
- standard deduction if they applied it;
- deductions they had proof for (such as declared investments);
- tax on the remainder, including cess, minus rebate if they applied one;
- TDS already deducted.

This is why [CTC vs take-home](/blog/ctc-vs-take-home-salary-in-india) arguments belong in the offer stage, and Form 16 belongs at filing time. CTC will not appear as a line you can file.

<Callout type="example" title="Two employers, one year">
Illustrative only. You leave a job in August and join another in September.

You should receive **two** Form 16s. Each Part A shows TDS that employer deposited. Your ITR must add **both** salaries. If the second employer did not get the first Form 16, they may have deducted too little; the ITR then shows the balance as payable.
</Callout>

## Matching the three documents

Work in this order:

1. **Payslips** — monthly gross, exemptions, TDS.
2. **Form 16** — annual totals from that employer.
3. **26AS / AIS** — what the department says was deposited against your PAN, plus other information.

If payslip TDS sums to ₹1.2 lakh and 26AS shows ₹1.0 lakh, the missing ₹20,000 is not “rounding”. Ask payroll. If you file using Form 16’s higher credit, processing can reduce it.

AIS may show interest the bank reported. Form 16 will not. Leaving it out is how notices begin.

## Regime, proofs and what payroll cannot see

Payroll can only deduct on the proofs you submitted in their window, and on the regime you asked them to use. If you later change regime in the ITR, or you forgot a donation receipt, **your return** is the place — not a handwritten edit on Form 16.

If you opted for a regime with fewer deductions, Part B may show almost no 80C-style lines. That can be correct.

<Callout type="verify">
Form layout, due dates for issuing Form 16, and which exemptions exist in each regime change. Confirm on [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in) for the assessment year you are filing.
</Callout>

## What Form 16 is not

- It is **not** proof of your full income.
- It is **not** a refund order.
- It is **not** GST.
- It does not fix a wrong PAN used on an FD.

Digital signature: a Form 16 from TRACES / the employer’s system should be verifiable. A blurry photo from a group chat is not a certificate.

<Callout type="opinion">
We think you should open Form 16 the week you get it, not the night before the ITR due date. Discrepancies need payroll time. The portal will not wait for your HR ticket.
</Callout>

## Filing checklist

1. All Form 16 PDFs for the year.
2. 26AS and AIS downloaded on the same day you start the return.
3. Interest certificates, capital-gains statements, rent receipts if you claim what the law still allows.
4. Bank account for refund — the one you actually use, with the IFSC on [the statement](/blog/how-to-read-a-bank-statement).
5. If numbers clash, pause filing and write to the deductor; do not “average” two documents.
