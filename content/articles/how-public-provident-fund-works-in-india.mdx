The **Public Provident Fund (PPF)** is a government small-savings scheme: you deposit money each financial year, it earns a notified interest rate, and the account runs for a long tenure. It is a **savings** product with a lock, not a market investment. The rate can change for future periods; the government notifies small-savings rates, it does not guarantee last quarter’s number forever.

This article explains the mechanics. It does not say PPF is “better than” a mutual fund or an FD.

<KeyTakeaways>
- A PPF account is typically a **15-year** contract, extendable in blocks, at a post office or an authorised bank.
- You must deposit **at least the minimum** and not more than the **annual ceiling** in a financial year (figures are in the scheme rules and can be revised).
- Interest is **notified** and credited as the rules specify; do not confuse the latest press release with a lifelong rate.
- **Loans and partial withdrawals** are allowed only in specified years, with limits.
- Tax treatment has historically been favourable, but **deductions depend on the tax regime you use**. Confirm for the current year.
</KeyTakeaways>

## Opening and who it is for

Resident individuals can open a PPF, including for a minor, subject to the scheme’s one-account rules. Non-resident status later can restrict deposits; read the rule before you assume you can keep funding after you move abroad.

You open it with KYC at a **bank branch** that is a PPF agency or at a **post office**. The passbook or e-passbook is the record of deposits and interest.

PPF fits money you can lock for a long horizon: retirement top-up, a child’s education that is more than a decade away, or a forced savings habit. It is a poor [emergency fund](/blog/emergency-fund-how-much-should-you-save).

## Deposits through the year

The scheme sets a **minimum** deposit in a financial year to keep the account active, and a **maximum** across all your PPF deposits in that year (including a minor’s account you fund, where the rules club them). Excess deposits can be rejected or treated as unnamed; do not try to “park” a bonus by overshooting the cap.

You can deposit in lump or in instalments, up to the number of deposits the rules allow. Interest for a month typically depends on the **lowest balance** in a defined window of that month. Timing deposits before the 5th of the month is a common operational habit — confirm the current rule in the passbook terms rather than a social-media mnemonic.

<Callout type="verify">
Minimum and maximum yearly deposits, the day-of-month rule for interest, and extension conditions are in the PPF Scheme as amended. Check [nsiindia.gov.in](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in) or your bank/post office circular for the year you are depositing.
</Callout>

## Interest

The Ministry of Finance notifies small-savings interest rates, usually for a quarter. Your PPF earns at the rate that applies to that period under the notification. Compounding is built into the scheme (interest is added to the balance as specified).

Do not compare a PPF rate from a screenshot with a mutual fund’s **past** one-year return. One is a notified deposit-style rate; the other is a market outcome that can reverse.

## Liquidity: loan, withdrawal, premature closure

PPF is not a savings account.

- **Loan:** after a specified number of years from the end of the year of opening, you may borrow a portion of the balance, at an interest rate the scheme sets, until you repay.
- **Partial withdrawal:** after a later specified year, you may withdraw a portion, with limits on how much and how often.
- **Premature closure:** allowed only in listed situations (such as certain medical or education needs, or after a minimum number of years), often with an interest **discount**. It is not an ATM.

If you need a dated rupee amount in three years, an [RD](/blog/how-recurring-deposits-work) or FD is the closer tool.

## Tax

PPF has long been discussed as **exempt-exempt-exempt**: deposits may qualify for a deduction under the old regime’s section for specified savings (subject to the overall cap), interest is exempt, and maturity is exempt — **when the law says so**.

Under the **new tax regime**, many deductions for this kind of deposit are not available. Interest and maturity treatment still need a current-year check. Do not contribute only “for 80C” if you have opted out of that deduction.

<Callout type="verify">
Deduction caps, regime rules and exemption of interest can change with the Finance Act. Confirm on the [Income Tax portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in) or with a tax professional. Our [income tax basics](/blog/basics-of-income-tax-in-india) explainer covers how regimes differ.
</Callout>

## Extension after 15 years

At maturity you can generally **withdraw fully** or **extend** in five-year blocks, with or without fresh deposits, if you apply as the scheme requires. Silent inaction can follow a default the bank or post office documents — read the maturity letter.

Nomination on the PPF form is separate from [bank account nomination](/blog/how-bank-account-nomination-works-in-india). Fill it.

## When PPF is a weak fit

- Money you might need for a medical bill next quarter.
- A goal that must beat inflation by a wide margin and can bear market risk — that is a different conversation, starting with [inflation](/blog/how-inflation-affects-your-savings) and [asset allocation](/blog/what-asset-allocation-means-for-beginners).
- Duplicate accounts or deposits above the annual ceiling.

<Callout type="opinion">
We think PPF works best as a **boring ceiling**: deposit an amount you will not miss, up to the cap if it still makes sense after tax-regime maths, and ignore the quarterly rate chatter. The product’s job is to stay untouched, not to win a one-year return contest.
</Callout>

## Opening checklist

1. Open in your own name (or as guardian for a minor) at a convenient agency, not five unfinished accounts.
2. Nomination and KYC.
3. Standing instruction sized for a lean month, not for the ceiling on day one.
4. Calendar reminder for the yearly minimum if you do not use auto-debit.
5. Passbook download once a year; match deposits to bank statements.
